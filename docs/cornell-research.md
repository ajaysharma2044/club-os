# Cornell Entrepreneurship Club: research and product decisions

Research date: September 11, 2026. Public-source research, not a claim of university endorsement.

## Organization identity

The relevant organization is **Cornell Entrepreneurship Club (CEC)** at
[www.cornellec.com](https://www.cornellec.com/). Its own website describes founder
events, building startups, and practical venture education. The site labels its
Engineering Fellowship as upcoming and unconfirmed; the application must not
advertise it as an available program.

This is distinct from Cornell Venture and Entrepreneurship Club (CVEC), Cornell
Venture Capital, and Cornell's MBA entrepreneurship organizations. Similar names
are not grounds for merging their rosters, events, sponsors or claims.

## Recruitment

[CEC's recruitment page](https://www.cornellec.com/recruitment) lists Events, Media,
and Generalist tracks and a Google Forms application link. It describes twice-yearly
recruitment. Its displayed September timeline does not state a year clearly enough
to establish the current cycle, so we do not import its dates as live deadlines.

Product decisions: configurable applications, restricted officer review, explicit
membership acceptance, and individually bookable coffee chats. The new application's
submission stays in Club OS and does not silently submit to CEC's existing form.

## Startup Hours

[Cornell's official October 2, 2025 listing](https://eship.cornell.edu/event/startup-hours/)
identifies CEC as host and Entrepreneurship at Cornell as funder. It describes a
student-run gathering for project work, advice, startup resources, and conversations
with builders and investors. The listing is historical: it does not establish a
future date, current sponsor agreement, or an investor's willingness to buy data.

Product decisions: public event visibility, authenticated RSVPs, capacity and waitlist,
explicit attendance adjudication, calendar export, and source-linked event records.
Officers must supply future dates and confirm venues. Missing attendance is never
converted into a negative personal outcome.

## Campus integration

[The official eHub page](https://eship.cornell.edu/item/ehub/) identifies Kennedy Hall
and Collegetown locations and links to access requests and room reservations.
Multi-purpose room reservations require staff approval. These are external processes;
an event in Club OS or an ICS calendar entry is not a confirmed room booking.

[How to launch a startup at Cornell](https://eship.cornell.edu/cornell-startups/how-to-launch-a-startup-at-cornell/)
organizes university resources by stage, from exploring ideas to launching and scaling.
The workspace links to this guide instead of inventing eligibility or funding offers.

## What was not established

Public research did not establish a current CEC roster, officer permissions, internal
Slack history, current operating budget, access to university APIs, a confirmed $100k
VC contract, or permission to publish member profiles. No such claims or data were
seeded. Initial training content is explicitly a configurable product template, not
CEC's official curriculum. No public attendee list was harvested.
